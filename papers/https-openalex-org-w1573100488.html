Dynamics of the manufacturer‐supplier relationships in emerging markets

Year: 2008

Primary subgenre: business

Secondary subgenres: economics

Summary: Purpose The purpose of this paper is to analyze the nature and the evolution of manufacturer–supplier relationships in Thailand's automobile industry and to identify the factors that influence the evolution of these relationships. Design/methodology/approach The study is based on case research method involving in‐depth interviews with 120 local and expatriates of 7 companies and their 14 suppliers
