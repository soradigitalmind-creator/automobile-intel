Symbolic Computation Using Cellular Automata-Based Hyperdimensional Computing

Year: 2015

Primary subgenre: artificial intelligence

Secondary subgenres: algorithmartificial neural networkfeature

Summary: This letter introduces a novel framework of reservoir computing that is capable of both connectionist machine intelligence and symbolic computation. A cellular automaton is used as the reservoir of dynamical systems. Input is randomly projected onto the initial conditions of automaton cells, and nonlinear computation is performed on the input via application of a rule in the automaton for a period
