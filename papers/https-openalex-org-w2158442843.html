Electric load forecasting using an artificial neural network

Year: 1991

Primary subgenre: artificial intelligence

Secondary subgenres: artificial neural networkelectrical engineeringmachine learning

Summary: An artificial neural network (ANN) approach is presented for electric load forecasting. The ANN is used to learn the relationship among past, current and future temperatures and loads. In order to provide the forecasted load, the ANN interpolates among the load and temperature data in a training data set.
